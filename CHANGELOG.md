# Changelog

## 0.3.3
- Settings and quick settings windows are bigger (most of the screen) and scroll when needed.

## 0.3.2
- Faster start after sign-in: optional installer choice removes Windows' ~10 second startup-app delay; the bar's own startup no longer waits on registry checks.

## 0.3.1
- Only one Everything Bar runs; starting it again just brings the running one forward.
- Start with Windows is repaired automatically (missing entry, switched off by Windows, or pointing at an old copy) unless you turned it off yourself. The installer also switches Windows' own startup toggle on.

## 0.3.0
- Stricter freeze: waits for controller buttons to be released before waking or opening an app; the app you left stays paused until you switch back (can be turned off); paused apps are marked on the bar.
- Fixed a hang caused by switching to a paused window.
- New quick settings panel: volume, on-screen keyboard, gamepad cursor, shortcuts to Network, Bluetooth, Airplane mode and Sound, power options.
- Lower idle CPU use.
- Renamed to Everything Bar; desktop shortcut option; complete uninstall (program, startup entry, optional saved data).
